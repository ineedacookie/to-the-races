archive. `purple.png`, the visibly filled `water.png`, and the
`growth.png`, `shrink.png`, and `transform.png` bottles are palette-swapped
derivatives of `blue.png` generated by `scripts/prepare_potion_assets.py`;
they remain available under CC0.
