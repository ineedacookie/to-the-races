# Third-party assets

All third-party art and audio is stored locally. None is hotlinked at runtime.

## Project-original graphics

The grandstand styling, crowd reaction UI, and Phaser procedural textures for bananas
and potholes are **original to this project** (drawn in code/CSS, not sourced from
external packs). The betting sheet also uses Unicode symbols for those track items,
rendered by the visitor's system font; no emoji font or emoji artwork is distributed
with the app. These elements should not be attributed to the artists below.

## Fantasy racers

**Monsters Creatures Fantasy** and **Monsters Creatures Fantasy 2** by LuizMelo

- Primary sources:
  - https://luizmelo.itch.io/monsters-creatures-fantasy
  - https://luizmelo.itch.io/monsters-creatures-fantasy-2
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain
- Local files: `static/assets/racers/sheets/`
- Source mirror used for the local copies:
  https://github.com/dowoonlee/ai-service-usage

The mirror's strips preserve the original run/walk/flight frames while trimming
transparent canvas space. `scripts/prepare_assets.py` validates their frame grids,
generates first-frame betting portraits, and writes SHA-256 checksums to
`static/assets/racers/manifest.json`. The original source and mirror license notices
are retained in `static/assets/racers/LICENSES.md`.

Characters used:

- Skeleton, Mushroom, Goblin, and Flying Eye from Monsters Creatures Fantasy
- Mimic, Rat, Slime, and Bat from Monsters Creatures Fantasy 2

The first group races on the track. The CC0 Mimic, Rat, Slime, and Bat portraits
appear as the four grandstand mascots.

## Pixel potion drinks

**Potions Pack** by FunnyDude

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/potions-pack
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain
- Local files: `static/assets/items/potions/`
- Source archive SHA-256:
  `b1794e14a9816f26f02e1c6ede6906c97dc2800a3ac8a03717ce6bf182f2788e`

The blue, green, red, and empty 16×16 sprites are unmodified source files. The
purple drink is a palette-swapped derivative of the blue sprite, generated by
`scripts/prepare_potion_assets.py`. Detailed file checksums are stored beside the
assets.
